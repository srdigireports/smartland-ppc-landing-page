Common questions

child care FAQs

How much does child care cost, and do you accept CCS?
Yes, we're an approved Child Care Subsidy provider. Our daily fee includes meals, snacks, wipes and sunscreen. Call us for current fees and we'll explain how your CCS applies.
What are your opening hours?
Monday to Friday, 6:30am to 6:30pm, 52 weeks a year. We're closed on weekends and public holidays.
What ages do you care for?
From 6 weeks to school age, across Nursery, Toddlers, Pre Kindy and Kindergarten rooms.
Do you offer Free Kindy?
Yes. We run a NSW Government approved kindergarten program led by a university-qualified early childhood teacher, and it's Free Kindy eligible.
Do all staff hold a blue card?
Yes. Every staff member, student and volunteer holds a valid NSW blue card (Working With Children Check) before they start.
Who can collect my child?
Only adults you've pre-approved. Every drop-off and pickup is recorded with our digital sign-in and sign-out system.
What if my child becomes unwell at the centre?
We'll call you straight away and keep your child comfortable and away from other children until you arrive. Our illness and exclusion policy is shared with every family at enrolment.
Is there a waitlist?
Availability changes by room and day. Call us on or book a visit and we'll check current vacancies for you.
